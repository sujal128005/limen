# Limen brand

**Chosen (19 Nov 2026): option B, deep emerald.**

- Primary (arch, buttons, links): emerald `#174A40`. Darker hover `#0F3830`. Dark-mode emerald `#4FB89C`; light arch on dark backgrounds `#5FBFA3`.
- Accent (the step, highlights): Nexara9 gold `#E2B862`. Light gold `#F0CF85`.
- Dark surfaces: deep green-black `#0E2420`.
- Text ink: `#0F1B2D`.

**The mark:** an arch (a doorway) in emerald with a shorter right leg, over a separate gold step with a slanted right end, drawn from the outer stroke of the Nexara9 logo. "Limen" is Latin for threshold: the arch is the doorway, the gold bar the step across it.
