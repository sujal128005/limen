**One-liner:** AI that buys for Indian manufacturers, within spending limits it cannot break.

**What are you building? (short)**
Limen is an AI purchasing platform for mid-sized Indian manufacturers. AI agents read the tender, shortlist qualified suppliers and negotiate the deal. The buyer's budget ceiling and each supplier's price floor are enforced by a smart contract that no agent, prompt or bug can change. People still approve and pay, and every step is on a tamper-evident record.

**Problem:** Purchasing is still the most manual job in a factory. Teams spend days reading tenders, chasing quotes and haggling by phone. AI can already do this work, but no finance team trusts it with money, because in today's tools the spending limit lives inside the same software the AI runs in. One clever prompt or one bug can overspend, so AI only drafts and people retype the order.

**Solution / why it's different:** We moved the limit out of the AI's software. The ceiling and floor are contract state, so even if our code or the model is wrong, the overspend is refused. SAP Ariba, Coupa, Zip, Pactum and Procol all keep the limit inside their own software.

**Traction / proof:** A live product runs a full deal end to end (sourced, approved, funded, shipped, received, paid). 600 automated tests; 25 of 25 attack scenarios on the limits blocked. Regional Winners and National Finalists, IBM SkillsBuild Hackathon 2026; Winners, RizeOS Hackathon 2026. Pre-revenue.

**Market:** India's MSME procurement economy is about ₹124.9 lakh crore (India SME Forum), and only 30–40% of it runs through digital channels. The global procurement software market is expected to reach $15.8bn by 2030.

**Business model:** 1.5% of settled deal value, charged only when an order is delivered and paid. Later: private deployments (subscriptions) and paid supplier tools.

**Go-to-market:** Mid-sized manufacturers buying resins, metals and packaging in Gujarat, Maharashtra, Tamil Nadu and Telangana, then the UAE and Southeast Asia.
