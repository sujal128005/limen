## Message drafts

LinkedIn connection notes are capped at **300 characters**. Send the short note first, then the longer message once they accept.

**Connection note (investors, ≤300 chars)**
> Hi {Name}, I'm Sujal, building Limen: an AI agent that sources and negotiates B2B purchases while a smart contract caps what it can spend. Working demo is live. Given your work in {their focus}, would love to connect and get your take on our pre-seed plans.

**Follow-up (investor)**
> Hello {Name}!
>
> Thanks for connecting. {One line on something specific: their thesis, a portfolio company, a post.}
>
> We're a small founding team building Limen, an AI-powered B2B procurement platform. A buyer's AI agent sources suppliers and negotiates, but the spending limit lives in a smart contract the agent cannot change. It can negotiate a deal; it can't overspend. We have a working two-sided demo (buyer desk + supplier portal): https://limen-peqe.onrender.com
>
> We're preparing for a pre-seed round and would value your view on two things:
> 1. What would you need to see from a team at our stage (pilots, LOIs, a specific metric) before a first cheque?
> 2. Does Limen fit what you're investing in now, and if not, who would you point us to?
>
> Happy to share our deck, or show you a 10-minute demo, whichever is easier.
>
> Regards,
> Sujal Negi, Team Limen

**Eximius-specific opener:** "Your 'Just an Agent Away' piece on hybrid autonomy is close to how we think about Limen. The agent negotiates, but money moves only with human approval and inside a limit it can't touch."

**Srinath / Asish / procurement operators (advice first, not money):**
> …You've built one of India's biggest B2B procurement businesses, so I'd love your honest take: when SME buyers adopt new procurement tools, what makes them trust it with real orders? And what would a convincing first pilot look like?
