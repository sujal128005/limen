From a tender to a paid order, in one place.

This is Limen, the first venture from Nexara9: AI-powered purchasing for India's manufacturers.

Here is one purchase from our demo, start to finish:

1️⃣ Read the tender: paste a requirement or upload the tender PDF. Limen picks out the material, grade, quantity, certificates, delivery and budget, and asks when something does not add up.
2️⃣ Match suppliers: only suppliers that meet every requirement go through, compared on what you would actually pay, with GST and freight included.
3️⃣ Approve: the purchase head approves, and the money is held safely until the goods arrive.
4️⃣ Ship and get paid: the supplier ships and is paid on delivery. No chasing invoices.

Buyers get a shortlist in minutes instead of days of calls. Suppliers get orders without cold calls, and get paid on time.
