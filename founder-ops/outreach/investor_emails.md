**What Limen does**
Purchasing is still the most manual job in an Indian factory. Teams spend days reading tenders, chasing quotes and haggling. AI can already do this work, but no business trusts it with real money: today the spending limit lives inside the same software the AI runs in.

Limen fixes that. Our AI agents read the tender, shortlist qualified suppliers and negotiate the deal. The buyer's budget ceiling and each supplier's price floor are enforced by a smart contract that no agent, prompt or bug can change. People still approve and pay, and every step is on a tamper-evident record.

**Where we are**
- **A live, working product.** A full deal already runs end to end: sourced, approved, funded, shipped, received, paid.
- **Built to hold.** 600 automated tests, and 25 of 25 attack scenarios against the spending limits blocked.
- **Recognised by judges.** Regional Winners and National Finalists at the IBM SkillsBuild Hackathon 2026, and Winners of the RizeOS Hackathon 2026, both with Limen.
- **Next step.** First pilots with mid-sized buyers of resins, metals and packaging in Gujarat, Maharashtra, Tamil Nadu and Telangana. We started in 2026 and are pre-revenue.

**Business model:** 1.5% of settled deal value, charged only when an order is delivered and paid.

**The ask:** we're raising a ₹1.5 crore pre-seed round to run our first pilots, onboard suppliers and grow the team.
